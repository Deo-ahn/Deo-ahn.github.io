A wearable **SLAM-based smart helmet** designed to help firefighters maintain spatial awareness inside smoke-filled, GPS-denied environments. The system combined onboard sensing with simultaneous localization and mapping to provide reliable trajectory information.

This project, along with indoor drone trajectory tracking, sparked my interest in **reliable real-world AI systems** and motivated my later move toward video understanding and anomaly detection.

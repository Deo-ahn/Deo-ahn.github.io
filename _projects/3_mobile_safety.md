Building on the surveillance program above, this project — _"Development of a Mobile-Platform-Based Solution for Active Safety Services"_ — extends anomaly understanding toward **mobile, proactive safety scenarios**. It is a government-funded program of **≈ US$9.5 million over ~5 years**, carried out in collaboration with the **Korean National Police Agency (KNPA)** and the **Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy (MOTIE)**.

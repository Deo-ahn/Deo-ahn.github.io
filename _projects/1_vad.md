At the **Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST)**, I study **video anomaly detection** in surveillance scenarios with a focus on modeling **contextual dynamics** — integrating RGB appearance, motion dynamics, and scene information so that the model better captures _anomaly concepts through their relationships_.

Recent work explores VLM-guided unsupervised approaches with adaptive sampling for efficiency and target-domain transfer.

01Research Interests

Video understanding, contextual dynamics, and efficient AI systems for social good.

My research interests center on video understanding, contextual dynamics, and efficient AI systems for social good. Beginning with sensing applications, including an ultrasonic SLAM helmet for firefighters and indoor drone tracking, I became interested in dependable systems for real settings. I now study video anomaly understanding, action recognition, and vision language models that explain anomalous behavior reliably and efficiently.

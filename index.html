Hello there!

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Brain & Cognitive Engineering at Korea University and a student researcher at KIST AI·Robotics. My research interests center on video models that understand what happens, where it happens, and why it matters. I study how to make these systems reliable and efficient in the real world.

My research explores video anomaly understanding, contextual dynamics, action recognition, and efficient vision language systems. Starting from sensing applications, I became interested in dependable AI systems that remain useful under the ambiguity and constraints of real deployments.

Recent work includes BUSTER for anomaly detection guided by VLMs with adaptive sampling, Look Closer for detailed proficiency estimation, and Where and What for contextual surveillance video understanding.

Current focus: why vision language models hallucinate in video anomaly understanding, and how selective visual verification can make their explanations more faithful.
